# Tata Data Visualisation Project

## About the Project

This project was completed as part of the Tata Data Visualisation job simulation.

The objective was to analyse an online retail dataset and provide insights that could help the CEO and CMO make business and expansion decisions.

## Tools Used

- Power BI
- Excel
- Data Cleaning
- Data Visualisation

## Data Cleaning

The dataset was cleaned by:

- Removing records where Quantity was less than 1
- Removing records where Unit Price was less than 0
- Calculating Revenue using Quantity × Unit Price

## Analysis

### Question 1
Monthly Revenue for 2011 was analysed using a line chart to identify seasonal trends.

### Question 2
The Top 10 countries by revenue and quantity sold were analysed, excluding the United Kingdom.

### Question 3
The Top 10 customers by revenue were identified and sorted from highest to lowest.

### Question 4
Product demand by country was visualised using a map, excluding the United Kingdom.

## Key Business Insight

Countries with strong revenue and high product demand can be considered potential opportunities for future expansion.
